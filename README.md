# Instacart Customer Segmentation Analysis 🛒

An exploratory data analysis of Instacart's 2022 grocery shopping dataset to uncover customer behavior patterns and inform targeted marketing strategies.

---

## 🎯 Objective

Instacart sought to improve their **targeted marketing strategies**. As the analyst, my task was to uncover sales patterns and deliver actionable segmentation insights, investigating:

- Busiest shopping times (by day and hour)
- Times of highest spending
- Product popularity across departments
- Behavioral differences across customer segments (age, region, family status, income)

---

## 📌 Key Findings

- **Order Volume Peaks:** Highest order volume on weekends, particularly 10 AM–2 PM.
- **Spending Peaks:** Average order value peaks in the evenings (5–8 PM) — optimal ad placement window.
- **Product Popularity:** Produce and dairy consistently rank as the most purchased categories.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Clear behavioral patterns identified across loyalty levels, family statuses, and income brackets.
- **Loyalty Indicators:** High-frequency users show consistent repeat behavior — strong candidates for loyalty program incentives.

- **Segment campaigns** based on identified customer loyalty and demographic profiles
